Cub Scouting

Ranks, dens and advancement

Cub Scouting is for kids in kindergarten through 5th grade. Each grade is a rank, and scouts move up every year with their den.

Ranks by grade

Dens and meetings

Each grade has a boys' den and a girls' den. Dens meet on their own schedule with parent volunteer leaders. Once a month, every den and family comes together for the pack meeting, run by the Cubmaster and the pack committee.

Advancement

Every rank, from Lion to Arrow of Light, takes six required adventures and at least two electives. Lions through Bears earn a belt loop for each adventure. Webelos and Arrow of Light scouts earn pins.

Your den leader records advancement in Scoutbook. Ask them how they want adventures done at home reported. Full adventure requirements for every rank are on CubScouts.org.

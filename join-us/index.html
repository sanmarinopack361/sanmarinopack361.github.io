Dens by grade

Your scout joins a small den of kids in the same grade, led by parent volunteers.

Den and pack meetings

Families are expected at their den meetings and at the monthly pack meeting (next one: Friday, December 4 at 6 pm, K.L. Carver Auditorium). Dens set their own schedule and share it on BAND.

Kindergarten

Lions

Kindergartners join as Lions, with a parent at every meeting as their Lion Guide. Lions can wear either the Lion T-shirt or the blue Cub Scout uniform. Registration opens each August for kids starting kindergarten that fall.

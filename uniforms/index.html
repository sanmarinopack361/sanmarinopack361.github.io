Uniforms

What to wear

The uniform shows a scout belongs, puts every kid on equal footing, and gives them a place to show off what they've earned.

Kindergarten

Lion T-shirt or blue uniform

Lions can wear either the Lion T-shirt or the blue Cub Scout uniform. Check with your den leader which one your den uses.

1st to 4th grade

Blue uniform

Tiger, Wolf, Bear and Webelos scouts wear the blue Cub Scout uniform. Lions may wear it too.

5th grade

Tan and green uniform

Arrow of Light scouts wear the tan and green uniform.

Pack T-shirt

The pack T-shirt is worn on hikes, campouts and service projects. Shirts are $15 each for scouts, siblings and parents.

Order pack T-shirts →

Where the patches go

  • Left shoulder: Greater Los Angeles Area Council patch and the unit numerals 3, 6, 1
  • Above the left pocket: World Crest, with the World Crest ring
  • Right shoulder: US flag (comes sewn on the shirt), with the den number just below it. Check with your den leader before buying the den number.
  • Left pocket: badge of rank
  • Webelos: Webelos colors on the right shoulder, with adventure pins on the tabs

New uniforms often come with the council patch and World Crest already sewn on.

Uniform inspections

From time to time the pack holds a friendly uniform inspection at a pack meeting. It's about pride, not rules, and new families without a full uniform are welcome to join in.

Uniform exchange

Kids outgrow uniforms fast. Bring outgrown uniform pieces to any pack meeting, and ask for what you need by emailing info@sanmarinopack361.org with the item and size. A Scout is thrifty.

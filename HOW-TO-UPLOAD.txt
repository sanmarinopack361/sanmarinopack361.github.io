1. Unzip pack361-website.zip on your computer.
2. Go to github.com/sanmarinopack361/sanmarinopack361.github.io (signed in as pack361-treasurer or your own account).
3. Click "Add file" → "Upload files".
4. Open the unzipped pack361-website folder, select EVERYTHING inside it (all folders and files —
   not the pack361-website folder itself), and drag it onto the GitHub upload page.
   Wait until every file shows in the list.
5. Scroll down → "Commit changes". Files with the same name are replaced.
6. Wait 1–2 minutes (progress: the "Actions" tab; green check = live).
7. Open https://sanmarinopack361.github.io and press Shift+Reload (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac) to skip old cached files.

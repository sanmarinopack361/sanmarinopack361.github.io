Camping & Hiking

What to bring

For pack campouts and hikes, including the spring overnight at Lacy Park.

The Cub Scout Six Essentials

Every scout carries these on every hike and outing.

First aid kit

Bandages, moleskin, antibiotic ointment, pain reliever

Filled water bottle

Full before you leave

Flashlight

With fresh batteries

Trail food

Trail mix, granola bars, fruit. No candy.

Sun protection

Sunscreen, hat, lip balm

Whistle

Emergencies only. Three blasts means help.

Sleeping gear

Tent

A simple dome tent is best. Go a size bigger than you think you need, rather than a cramped multi-room tent.

Sleeping pad

Foam, self-inflating, or air mattress, from about $20 to $300.

Sleeping bag

Check the temperature rating. Synthetic or down fill; mummy, rectangular or hybrid shapes all work.

Clothing and more

  • Sneakers, hiking shoes or boots. No open-toed shoes.
  • Layers for cold nights
  • Insect repellent
  • Toiletries

Leave at home

  • Electronics (lock them in the car)
  • Large knives
  • Firearms and fireworks
  • Drugs, alcohol and tobacco
